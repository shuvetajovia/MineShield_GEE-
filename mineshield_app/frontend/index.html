📚 Terrain Risk Classification Guidelines

Slope Stability Rules

  • Low (< 5°): Stable, flat terrains. Minimal risk.
  • Moderate (5–15°): Monitor loose soil / overburden.
  • High (15–30°): Steep profile. Anchoring recommended.
  • Very High (> 30°): Critical failure risk. Evacuate active pits.

Topographic Elevation Risk

  • Low (< 400m): Low baseline potential energy.
  • Moderate (400–600m): standard bench slopes.
  • High (600–800m): Deep pit wall exposure.
  • Very High (> 800m): Max baseline height differential.

Spectral Indices (NDVI / Rock)

  • High NDVI (> 0.4): Heavily vegetated. Roots bind slope soil.
  • Low NDVI (< 0.15): Sparse vegetation. Bare soil exposure.
  • High Rock Exposure (> 60%): Highly fractured bare bedrock.

SAR backscatter (VV / VH)

  • Low stddev (< 0.5): Minimal surface deformation.
  • Moderate stddev (0.5–1.5): Standard operational movement.
  • High stddev (> 1.5): Significant ground displacement.
